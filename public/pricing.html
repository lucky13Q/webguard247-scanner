Questions, answered

Do you fix the problems for me?
No — WebGuard247 finds the issues and gives you clear, step-by-step instructions to fix each one (or hand to your developer). It's a diagnosis and an action plan, not a repair service.
What's the difference between the £4.99 guide and Pro?
The £4.99 Fix-It Guide is a one-time snapshot — it shows you how to fix everything wrong today, and it's yours to keep. Pro keeps watching your site for you, re-scanning every few days and emailing the moment something new slips, so you're covered over time rather than just once.
What does the monitoring actually do?
We re-check your site on a set schedule — every 3 days on Pro, daily on Agency — and watch for anything that changes: a certificate about to expire, a security setting that drops, or your site going unreachable. If something shifts, we email you so you can act before it becomes a problem.
Can I cancel whenever I like?
Yes. Plans are rolling and you can cancel any time from your account — you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
Is the scan safe to run on my site?
Completely. WebGuard247 is passive — it only inspects what any visitor's browser can already see. It never hacks, port-scans, logs in, or touches anything private.
